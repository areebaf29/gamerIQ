What each field means

Every input you provide is used as a feature by the ML models

⏱️

Playtime (hours/day)

How many hours you play games on an average day. This is the strongest signal for player type and addiction.

😴

Sleep (hours/day)

Hours of sleep you get. Low sleep combined with high playtime is a major addiction indicator.

📅

Gaming days/week

How many days per week you game. Affects engagement cluster classification.

🕹️

Game type

The genre you play most (FPS, RPG, MOBA etc.). Stored for analysis but rage patterns differ by genre.

😡

Rage level (1–10)

How often you rage/get angry while gaming. High rage + low sleep + high playtime = strong addiction signal.

📉

Productivity drop (%)

How much your work/study productivity has dropped due to gaming. Used in all three model predictions.
